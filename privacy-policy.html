Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Information We Collect

DividendStrike collects minimal information to operate and improve our website:

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, referral sources (via Google Analytics)
  • Local storage — market data caching and newsletter subscriptions stored in your browser

How We Use Your Information

  • To send newsletter updates (only if you subscribed)
  • To improve website content and user experience
  • To analyze traffic patterns through Google Analytics

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data including pages visited, time on site, device type, and geographic location. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies through Google Analytics. No personal tracking cookies are set by DividendStrike directly. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Data Storage

Newsletter subscriptions are stored in your browser's localStorage. We do not store your email on external servers. Market data from Twelve Data is cached locally to reduce API calls.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics
  • Twelve Data — real-time market data
  • Supabase — blog content hosting
  • Google Fonts — website typography

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time
  • Clear your browser's localStorage to remove cached data
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through our website.
