## ✨ Features

### 🔍 AI Dividend Screener
- Filter 40+ NSE stocks by dividend yield, growth rate, and sustainability
- AI Health Score based on 17 financial signals
- Real-time sorting and pagination

### 📊 Stock Analysis
- Detailed dividend payment history (6 years)
- AI growth predictions (3-year CAGR forecast)
- Sustainability scoring (FCF coverage, debt analysis)
- Promoter holding and FII/DII conviction tracking

### 🧮 DRIP Calculator
- Dividend reinvestment simulator with Indian tax brackets
- Project returns over 1-30 year horizons
- Monthly income projections
- Compare DRIP vs. cash payout scenarios

### ⚖️ Stock Comparison
- Compare up to 4 stocks side-by-side
- Metrics: Price, P/E, Yield, DPS, Growth, AI Score, Sustainability

### 💼 Portfolio Manager
- Track holdings with buy price and quantity
- Real-time P&L calculation
- Annual & monthly dividend income projection
- Local storage persistence

### 📝 Blog System
- SEO-optimized blog with Supabase backend
- Categories: Stock Lists, Strategy, Tax Guide, Tools
- Admin panel for content management
- Related posts and sharing features

### 🏆 Top Dividend Payers
- Ranked list of highest yield NSE stocks
- AI score + sustainability rating
- One-click stock detail view
